# Huong dan sua Pricing RapidAPI (5 phut)

Van de: Ca 3 goi (Basic/Pro/Mega) deu quota 500,000 req/thang.
=> Goi tra phi khong co gi hon goi free => khong ai tra tien.

## Cac buoc
1. Mo https://rapidapi.com/studio (dang nhap bang tai khoan tuyentn23).
2. Chon API: Invoice to JSON Extractor.
3. Vao tab Monetize (hoac Plans & Pricing).
4. Sua tung goi theo bang duoi.
5. Bam Save / Publish cho moi goi.
6. Kiem tra lai bang trang pricing public.

## Bang gia de xuat

| Goi | Gia | Quota | Rate limit | Ghi chu |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Basic (Free) | $0 | 500 req/thang | 5 req/s | Du de thu, het nhanh |
| Pro | $9.99/mo | 10,000 req/thang | 20 req/s | Cho startup |
| Mega | $49.00/mo | 100,000 req/thang | 50 req/s | Cho doanh nghiep |

## Vi sao cac con so nay
- Free 500 req = du de test 1-2 hoa don, KHONG du chay that.
- Pro 10k req = 1 doanh nghiep nho xu ly ~330 hoa don/ngay.
- Mega 100k req = quy mo lon.
- Overage: cho phep tra them ($0.002/req cho Pro) thay vi Hard Limit.

## Sau khi sua
- Goi free het quota se co nguoi nang cap => co doanh thu.
- Theo doi subscriber tren dashboard.
